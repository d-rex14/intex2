Lighthouse Sanctuary Motto

We are Lighthouse: full of hope, love and new beginnings. Our focus is progress in all aspects of life. We treat each other as family where each individual is seen, heard and loved. We create fun memories, we fight for justice and we acknowledge God in all we do.

Wheels of Hope:

A Van That Changes Lives

Every mile matters when a girl’s safety and future are on the line. Our Wheels of Hope campaign supports the purchase of a new van that does far more than provide transportation—it delivers security, dignity, and connection. The vans bring girls to safety, shuttle them to court hearings to seek justice, and take them to church and other community activities that help them build trust with safe, caring people. With reliable transportation, we can ensure that every girl has access to the protection, healing, and hope she deserves.
